Beebo's installer supplies its own integration scripts and original story templates.
It does not bundle Kokoro, its voice tensors, Python, PyTorch, Misaki or eSpeak-NG.
The optional voice setup installs/downloads those components on the user's PC.
Each dependency retains its own license and notices. eSpeak-NG has GPL terms;
its separate installation must not be described as an Apache-licensed component.
If a later Beebo installer bundles those dependencies, audit that exact bundle
and supply all required license, notice and source-code materials before release.

The upstream model card credits public-domain and permissive training data,
including Koniwa (CC BY 3.0) and SIWIS (CC BY 4.0). Preserve upstream notices
when redistributing model artifacts. This release offers the 28 English voices.
No custom voice-cloning feature or rights to a person's identity are included.

Beebo modifications: library provisioning, external worker dispatch, language-aware
English pipeline selection, CPU thread limit, error reporting and local audio caching.
