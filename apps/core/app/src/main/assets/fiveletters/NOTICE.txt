Five Letters word lists
=======================

answers.txt  - 1,527 everyday words that can be the hidden word
guesses.txt  - 3,625 more words accepted as guesses (the full allowed list is both files)

Source: the 12Dicts word lists by Alan Beale, as packaged in "Alt12Dicts"
version 2020.12.07 by Kevin Atkinson:
  https://github.com/en-wl/wordlist/tree/v1/alt12dicts
  http://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts/
Files used: 3esl.txt (answers pool), 2of12full.txt and 2of12id.txt (extra guesses).

Licence: public domain. The Alt12Dicts README states: "All of these files have been
explicitly placed in the Public Domain by Alan Beale." No attribution is required;
it is given here as a courtesy and so the source can be checked.

These lists were NOT taken from any other word game's lists.

Processing (apps/core/tools/fiveletters/build_wordlists.py): kept lowercase a-z
five-letter words only; removed slurs, sexual and crude words from both lists;
removed adult, gross, violent, drug/alcohol, religious, insulting, slang and
uncommon words from the answers (they are still accepted as guesses unless they
were in the blocked set); removed bare inflections (plurals, past tenses) from
the answers.
